# FrontDesk — Production Goal (first customer)

Get FrontDesk to a **first controlled pilot** with one real local-service business: it answers real
business calls, understands caller intent, uses that business's own context (hours/services/FAQs),
records booking/service requests, saves transcripts + summaries, and gives staff enough visibility to
trust it.

Pilot-ready, not perfect. It must be **usable, trustworthy, stable for a supervised pilot, honest about
its limits, understandable to a business owner, and able to show real value fast.**

Not required for first customer: enterprise scale, billing automation, full autonomy, multi-region.

Canonical detail: `reports/production-readiness-report.md` (audit), `PRODUCTION_TASKS.md` (P0/P1/P2),
`STATUS.md` (live status), `docs/deployment-checklist.md`, `docs/first-customer-onboarding.md`,
`docs/supabase-rls-verification.md`.
